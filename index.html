Key Insights
Analyst summary
  • East region generates ₹7.36L (41% of revenue) — more than double North, driven by Printers & Scanners.
  • Printers alone account for ₹9.34L — 52% of all revenue despite being only 20% of order volume. High-value item.
  • Siddhu leads the sales team at ₹5.52L with 96 orders — the only rep active across 3 regions, worth studying as a playbook.
  • Q3 (Jul–Sep) was the strongest quarter at ₹5.10L. February & September were the two top individual months.
  • Mouse, Keyboard, Speaker have high order frequency but low revenue impact — candidates for bundling with high-margin products.
  • North region underperforms in Printers (only ₹96,600 vs East's ₹3.61L) — the single highest-leverage growth opportunity.
